## Harmonised variables and code lists

- `h_hhkey` — None
- `h_head_sex` — Sex of household head (harmonised): 1 = Male; 2 = Female
- `h_head_age` — Age of household head (harmonised)
- `h_pkey` — None
- `h_sex` — Sex (harmonised): 1 male, 2 female: 1 = Male; 2 = Female
- `h_marital` — Marital status (harmonised, 4 groups): 1 = Never married; 2 = Married or in union (monogamous, polygamous, cohabiting); 3 = Divorced or separated; 4 = Widowed
- `h_relation` — Relationship to household head (harmonised, 5 groups): 1 = Head; 2 = Spouse; 3 = Child (own, step, adopted, foster); 4 = Other relative; 5 = Non-relative (incl. domestic worker, unknown)
- `h_attend` — Ever attended school (harmonised): 0 = Never attended school; 1 = Attended or attending
- `h_educ` — Highest level of education attended (harmonised, 4 levels): 0 = None or pre-primary; 1 = Primary; 2 = Secondary (post-primary, vocational, lower or upper secondary); 3 = Tertiary
- `h_literacy` — Can read and write (harmonised): 0 = Cannot read and write; 1 = Can read and write
- `h_lfstatus` — Labour-force status (harmonised codes; definition in h_lfs_def): 1 = Employed; 2 = Unemployed; 3 = Outside the labour force
- `h_employed` — Employed (harmonised; definition in h_lfs_def): 0 = Not employed; 1 = Employed
- `h_lfs_def` — Definition and age base behind h_lfstatus / h_employed: 1 = LFS: NISR status1, ILO 7-day; ages 16+ in 2017-2019, 14+ from 2020; 2 = Census 2002: activity situation over 15/07-15/08/2002; ages 6+; 3 = Census 2012: NISR rp2024, 7-day, relaxed unemployment (availability only); ages 5+; 4 = EICV1/EICV2: NISR econstatus, usual activity over 12 months; ages 7+ / 6+; 5 = EICV3/EICV5: any work in the last 12 months incl. own farm (no unemployment category in EICV3); ages 6+; 6 = EICV4: NISR lfs6, current status; ages 6+; 7 = EICV7 / AHS 2024: any work in the last 7 days incl. own farm, or temporarily absent; no unemployment category; ages 6+; 8 = AHS 2017/2020: main economic activity of members above 10
- `h_empstat` — Status in employment, main job (harmonised, 5 groups): 1 = Employee (incl. paid apprentice / intern); 2 = Employer; 3 = Own-account worker / self-employed; 4 = Contributing family worker; 5 = Other (cooperative member, unpaid apprentice, other)
- `h_isic1` — Industry of main job, ISIC Rev.4 section (harmonised 1-21): 1 = A Agriculture, forestry and fishing; 2 = B Mining and quarrying; 3 = C Manufacturing; 4 = D Electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning; 5 = E Water supply, sewerage, waste management; 6 = F Construction; 7 = G Wholesale and retail trade, repair of vehicles; 8 = H Transportation and storage; 9 = I Accommodation and food service; 10 = J Information and communication; 11 = K Financial and insurance activities; 12 = L Real estate; 13 = M Professional, scientific and technical; 14 = N Administrative and support services; 15 = O Public administration and defence; 16 = P Education; 17 = Q Human health and social work; 18 = R Arts, entertainment and recreation; 19 = S Other service activities; 20 = T Households as employers; 21 = U Extraterritorial organisations
- `h_isic1_approx` — h_isic1 from an older/national classification (approximate): 0 = Native ISIC Rev.4 1-digit; 1 = Approximate crosswalk (ISIC Rev.3 divisions or the EICV1/2 national groups)
- `h_isco1` — Occupation of main job, ISCO-08 major group (harmonised 0-9): 0 = Armed forces occupations; 1 = Managers; 2 = Professionals; 3 = Technicians and associate professionals; 4 = Clerical support workers; 5 = Service and sales workers; 6 = Skilled agricultural, forestry and fishery workers; 7 = Craft and related trades workers; 8 = Plant and machine operators and assemblers; 9 = Elementary occupations
- `h_isco1_approx` — h_isco1 from an older/national classification (approximate): 0 = Native ISCO-08 1-digit; 1 = Approximate crosswalk (ISCO-88 major groups or the EICV1/2 national groups)
- `h_head_marital` — Marital status of household head (harmonised, 4 groups): 1 = Never married; 2 = Married or in union (monogamous, polygamous, cohabiting); 3 = Divorced or separated; 4 = Widowed
- `h_head_educ` — Education of household head (harmonised, 4 levels): 0 = None or pre-primary; 1 = Primary; 2 = Secondary (post-primary, vocational, lower or upper secondary); 3 = Tertiary
- `h_head_literacy` — Household head can read and write (harmonised): 0 = Cannot read and write; 1 = Can read and write
